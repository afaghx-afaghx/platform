# Code of Conduct

AFAGHX is an engineering project built around respectful, evidence-based collaboration.

Expected behavior:
- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- critique code, architecture, and evidence rather than people;
- disclose security concerns responsibly;
- keep discussions factual and actionable;
- respect maintainers, contributors, and reviewers.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, doxxing, malicious disclosure, and repeated bad-faith participation.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors. Security-sensitive reports should follow `SECURITY.md`.
