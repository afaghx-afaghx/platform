# AFAGHX Product Taxonomy v1

The homepage search category selector is a presentation registry for the initial 36 product categories supplied by the AFAGHX product taxonomy.

Each category has:
- a stable slug used by the search request as `category`;
- a Persian display name for the FA experience;
- an English display name for the EN experience;
- a parent group in the homepage taxonomy.

The Experience Layer does not own the authoritative domain taxonomy. The canonical API/Domain layer must become the source of truth when the product/catalog search contract is promoted from prototype to production.

Categories are grouped as Food & Consumer, Industry & Infrastructure, and Technology & Services on the homepage.
